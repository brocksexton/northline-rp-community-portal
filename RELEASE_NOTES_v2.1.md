# Northline RP Community Portal v2.1

## Focus pass: Tweeter

This update gives `/tweeter` its own self-contained social experience inspired by classic pre-acquisition Twitter styling while preserving the rest of the Northline portal on every other route.

### Included
- Dedicated Tweeter route layout wrapper.
- Tweeter-specific full-page theme.
- Site header/footer hidden while viewing Tweeter.
- Three-column social layout:
  - left rail navigation,
  - central timeline,
  - right rail trends/search/suggestions.
- Top timeline tabs (`For you` and `Latest`).
- Richer tweet cards with replies, reposts, likes, and quoted-retweet display.
- Search and hashtag filtering.
- “Who to follow” suggestions derived from player activity.
- Feed stats and update time.
- Disabled composer UI to preserve read-only status until a secure bridge is implemented.

## Notes
- This remains read-only by design.
- A future bridge can enable posting, notifications, following, replies, and likes from the web safely.
