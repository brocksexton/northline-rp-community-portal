# Northline RP Community Portal v2.9.15

## Tweeter content filtering

- Added a first-pass Tweeter content filter for common profanity and slurs.
- Offending terms are now visually obscured with a soft blur mask and subtle sparkle treatment.
- Tweets with obscured text now show a small notice explaining why content was hidden.
- Users can hover over a masked term to peek at a small revealed area.
- Users can click a masked term to reveal the full word, and click again to hide it.
- Filtered rendering is now applied across the main Tweeter feed, profile timelines, thread cards, and full tweet detail pages.
