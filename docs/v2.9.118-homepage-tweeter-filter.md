# v2.9.118 — Homepage Tweeter safety filter

## Changed
- The homepage `Fresh from Tweeter` section now excludes posts containing terms covered by the Tweeter profanity/slur filter.
- The preview uses the first three safe posts instead of displaying the latest three posts raw.
- The homepage checks both the tweet body and the in-game Tweeter display name before showing a post.
- The homepage also includes a small extra blocklist for common plural slur variants, so the preview is stricter than the in-feed obscured-word presentation.

## Notes
- This affects only the homepage preview.
- Tweeter itself still handles filtered terms through its existing obscured-word presentation.
- Staff can continue managing filter terms from the Tweeter moderation panel.
